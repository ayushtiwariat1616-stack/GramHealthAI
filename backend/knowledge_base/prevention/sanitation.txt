# Topic: Sanitation

ID: PRE006

Category: Prevention

---

## Overview

Good sanitation helps prevent the spread of diseases by ensuring safe disposal of human waste, proper garbage management, and clean surroundings.

Poor sanitation increases the risk of diarrhea, cholera, typhoid, intestinal worm infections, and other illnesses.

---

## Good Sanitation Practices

Always:

- Use clean toilets.
- Wash hands after using the toilet.
- Dispose of waste properly.
- Keep drains clean.
- Prevent stagnant water.
- Maintain clean surroundings.

---

## Safe Waste Disposal

- Separate household waste when possible.
- Dispose of garbage in covered bins.
- Avoid throwing waste into rivers or ponds.
- Follow local waste collection guidelines.

---

## Safe Toilet Practices

- Always use a toilet if available.
- Keep toilets clean.
- Ensure children also use toilets safely.
- Clean toilets regularly using disinfectants.

---

## Community Hygiene

Communities should:

- Participate in cleanliness drives.
- Report blocked drains.
- Prevent open dumping of waste.
- Promote hygiene education.

---

## Diseases Prevented

Good sanitation helps prevent:

- Cholera
- Typhoid
- Diarrhea
- Hepatitis A
- Worm infections

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is sanitation important?

It prevents disease transmission and improves community health.

### Is handwashing part of sanitation?

Yes. Hand hygiene and sanitation work together to reduce infections.
