# Topic: Mosquito Control

ID: PRE004

Category: Prevention

---

## Overview

Mosquito control helps prevent diseases such as malaria, dengue, chikungunya, and Japanese encephalitis. Reducing mosquito breeding sites and protecting yourself from mosquito bites are the most effective prevention methods.

Community participation plays an important role in controlling mosquito-borne diseases.

---

## Prevent Mosquito Breeding

Reduce breeding by:

- Emptying water from coolers every week.
- Covering water storage containers.
- Cleaning water tanks regularly.
- Removing old tyres and containers that collect rainwater.
- Keeping drains clean.
- Preventing stagnant water around the house.

---

## Protect Yourself

Use:

- Mosquito nets while sleeping.
- Mosquito repellents.
- Full-sleeved clothing.
- Window and door screens.
- Fans where possible.

---

## During Dengue Season

Take extra precautions by:

- Checking your surroundings every week.
- Removing standing water.
- Wearing protective clothing during the day.
- Keeping the house clean.

---

## Community Measures

Communities should:

- Participate in cleanliness drives.
- Report mosquito breeding sites.
- Support local fogging programmes when conducted.
- Spread awareness about mosquito prevention.

---

## Diseases Prevented

Mosquito control helps prevent:

- Malaria
- Dengue
- Chikungunya
- Japanese Encephalitis
- Zika virus infection (where applicable)

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do mosquitoes breed only in dirty water?

No. Many mosquitoes, including those that spread dengue, breed in clean, stagnant water.

### When are dengue mosquitoes most active?

Aedes mosquitoes usually bite during the daytime, especially in the early morning and late afternoon.
