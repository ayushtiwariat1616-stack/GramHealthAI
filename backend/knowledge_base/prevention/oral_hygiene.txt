# Topic: Oral Hygiene

ID: PRE007

Category: Prevention

---

## Overview

Oral hygiene means keeping the mouth, teeth, and gums clean to prevent tooth decay, gum disease, bad breath, and other dental problems.

Good oral health also supports overall health.

---

## Daily Oral Care

Brush your teeth:

- Twice a day.
- For at least two minutes.
- Using fluoride toothpaste.
- With a soft-bristled toothbrush.

---

## Additional Oral Care Tips

- Clean between teeth using dental floss or other recommended methods.
- Rinse your mouth after meals.
- Drink enough clean water.
- Replace your toothbrush every three to four months.

---

## Healthy Eating for Teeth

Choose:

- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Milk
- Cheese
- Nuts
- Plenty of water

Limit:

- Sugary drinks
- Sticky sweets
- Frequent sugary snacks

---

## Signs of Dental Problems

Consult a dentist if you have:

- Tooth pain
- Bleeding gums
- Swollen gums
- Bad breath that does not improve
- Loose teeth
- Mouth ulcers lasting more than two weeks

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk of dental disease by:

- Brushing regularly.
- Visiting a dentist for regular check-ups.
- Avoiding tobacco products.
- Limiting sugary foods and drinks.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I brush my teeth?

Brush twice daily using fluoride toothpaste.

### Does sugar cause cavities?

Frequent consumption of sugary foods and poor oral hygiene increase the risk of tooth decay.
