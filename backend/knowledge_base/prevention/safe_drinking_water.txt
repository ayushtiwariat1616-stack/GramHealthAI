# Topic: Safe Drinking Water

ID: PRE002

Category: Prevention

---

## Overview

Safe drinking water is essential for good health. Contaminated water can spread diseases such as cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A, diarrhea, and other infections.

Using clean and safe water greatly reduces the risk of water-borne diseases.

---

## Sources of Safe Water

Safe drinking water may come from:

- Treated municipal water supply.
- Properly maintained borewells.
- Protected wells.
- Purified or filtered water.
- Boiled water.

---

## How to Make Water Safer

You can improve water safety by:

- Boiling water for at least one minute.
- Using certified water filters.
- Chlorinating water when recommended.
- Storing water in clean, covered containers.

---

## Safe Storage

Always:

- Keep drinking water covered.
- Use clean containers.
- Avoid touching water with dirty hands.
- Clean storage containers regularly.

---

## Signs Water May Be Unsafe

Water may be unsafe if it has:

- Bad smell.
- Unusual color.
- Cloudiness.
- Visible dirt.
- Poor taste.

Even clear water may contain harmful germs.

---

## Diseases Prevented

Using safe drinking water helps prevent:

- Cholera
- Typhoid
- Diarrhea
- Hepatitis A
- Dysentery

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is boiling water effective?

Yes. Boiling kills most disease-causing germs.

### Can clear water still be unsafe?

Yes. Harmful bacteria and viruses may not be visible.
