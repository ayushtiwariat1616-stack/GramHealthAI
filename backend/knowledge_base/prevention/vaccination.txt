# Topic: Vaccination

ID: PRE005

Category: Prevention

---

## Overview

Vaccination is one of the most effective ways to prevent infectious diseases. Vaccines train the immune system to recognize and fight harmful germs without causing the disease itself.

Vaccination protects both the individual and the community by reducing the spread of infectious diseases.

---

## Benefits of Vaccination

Vaccination helps:

- Prevent serious diseases.
- Reduce hospitalizations.
- Protect infants and older adults.
- Prevent disease outbreaks.
- Save lives.

---

## Diseases Prevented

Vaccines help protect against diseases such as:

- Tuberculosis (BCG)
- Polio
- Measles
- Hepatitis B
- Diphtheria
- Pertussis (Whooping Cough)
- Tetanus
- COVID-19
- Influenza (for eligible groups)

---

## Childhood Vaccination

Children should receive vaccines according to the National Immunization Schedule recommended by the Government of India.

Parents should keep their child's vaccination record safe.

---

## Adult Vaccination

Some adults may also require vaccines depending on:

- Age
- Pregnancy
- Occupation
- Medical conditions
- Travel requirements

Consult a healthcare professional for advice.

---

## Common Side Effects

Mild side effects may include:

- Pain at the injection site
- Mild fever
- Tiredness
- Swelling or redness

These usually improve within a few days.

---

## When to Seek Medical Care

Seek immediate medical attention if a person develops:

- Difficulty breathing
- Severe allergic reaction
- High fever that does not improve
- Persistent seizures
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are vaccines safe?

Yes. Vaccines are carefully tested for safety and effectiveness before approval.

### Can vaccines cause the disease they prevent?

Most vaccines cannot cause the disease they are designed to prevent.
