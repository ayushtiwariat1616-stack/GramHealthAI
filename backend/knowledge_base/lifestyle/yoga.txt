# Topic: Yoga

ID: LIFE005

Category: Lifestyle

---

## Overview

Yoga is a practice that combines physical postures, breathing exercises, and relaxation techniques. Regular yoga may improve flexibility, balance, strength, mental well-being, and overall quality of life.

Yoga should be practiced safely and according to individual ability.

---

## Benefits of Yoga

Regular yoga may help:

- Improve flexibility
- Strengthen muscles
- Improve balance
- Reduce stress
- Improve sleep quality
- Support mental well-being
- Reduce back pain in some people

---

## Types of Yoga Practices

Yoga may include:

- Stretching exercises
- Balance poses
- Breathing exercises (Pranayama)
- Relaxation techniques
- Meditation

---

## Safety Tips

Practice safely by:

- Starting slowly.
- Wearing comfortable clothing.
- Avoiding painful movements.
- Staying hydrated.
- Learning from a qualified instructor if possible.

---

## Who Should Be Careful?

Consult a healthcare professional before starting yoga if you have:

- Recent surgery
- Severe joint problems
- Pregnancy with complications
- Heart disease
- Severe back pain

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can beginners practice yoga?

Yes. Beginners can start with simple poses and gradually increase difficulty.

### Does yoga replace medical treatment?

No. Yoga supports overall health but should not replace medical care or prescribed treatment.
