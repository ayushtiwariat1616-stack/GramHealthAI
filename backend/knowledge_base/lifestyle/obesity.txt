# Topic: Obesity

ID: LIFE002

Category: Lifestyle

---

## Overview

Obesity is a condition in which excess body fat increases the risk of health problems. It develops when a person regularly consumes more calories than the body uses.

Obesity can affect people of all ages and is associated with several long-term diseases.

---

## Risk Factors

Common causes include:

- Unhealthy diet
- Physical inactivity
- Lack of sleep
- Family history
- Certain medical conditions
- Some medicines

---

## Health Risks

Obesity increases the risk of:

- Type 2 diabetes
- High blood pressure
- Heart disease
- Stroke
- Sleep apnea
- Joint problems
- Certain cancers

---

## Healthy Weight Management

To maintain a healthy weight:

- Eat a balanced diet.
- Reduce sugary drinks.
- Limit processed foods.
- Exercise regularly.
- Sleep adequately.
- Drink enough water.

---

## Healthy Eating Tips

Choose:

- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Whole grains
- Lean proteins
- Healthy fats

Limit:

- Sugary drinks
- Fried foods
- Packaged snacks
- Excess sweets

---

## When to Consult a Doctor

Seek medical advice if:

- Weight continues to increase despite lifestyle changes.
- Obesity is affecting daily activities.
- You have diabetes, high blood pressure, or heart disease.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is obesity only caused by eating too much?

No. Genetics, hormones, medications, and lifestyle all contribute.

### Can regular exercise help?

Yes. Regular exercise combined with healthy eating supports weight management.
