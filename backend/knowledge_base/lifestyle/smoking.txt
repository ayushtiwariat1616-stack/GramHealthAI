# Topic: Smoking

ID: LIFE003

Category: Lifestyle

---

## Overview

Smoking is one of the leading causes of preventable disease and death worldwide. Tobacco smoke contains thousands of harmful chemicals that damage nearly every organ in the body.

Quitting smoking improves health at any age.

---

## Health Risks

Smoking increases the risk of:

- Lung cancer
- Mouth and throat cancer
- Heart disease
- Stroke
- Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
- Asthma attacks
- Pneumonia

---

## Effects on Others

Second-hand smoke can harm:

- Children
- Pregnant women
- Older adults
- People with asthma or heart disease

---

## Benefits of Quitting

Stopping smoking helps:

- Improve breathing
- Reduce coughing
- Lower blood pressure
- Improve heart health
- Reduce cancer risk
- Increase life expectancy

Health benefits begin within hours of quitting.

---

## Tips to Quit

Helpful steps include:

- Set a quit date.
- Remove cigarettes and tobacco products.
- Avoid smoking triggers.
- Seek support from family and friends.
- Consult a healthcare professional if needed.

---

## When to Seek Medical Advice

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- You find it difficult to quit.
- You develop persistent cough.
- You cough up blood.
- You experience chest pain or shortness of breath.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it too late to quit?

No. Quitting smoking improves health regardless of age.

### Is passive smoking harmful?

Yes. Second-hand smoke increases the risk of many diseases.
