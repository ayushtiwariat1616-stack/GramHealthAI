# Topic: Poisoning

ID: FA008

Category: First Aid

---

## Overview

Poisoning occurs when harmful substances enter the body through swallowing, breathing, skin contact, or injection. Common poisons include medicines, household cleaners, pesticides, chemicals, and poisonous plants.

Poisoning is a medical emergency.

---

## Symptoms

Symptoms depend on the poison but may include:

- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Stomach pain
- Difficulty breathing
- Dizziness
- Confusion
- Burns around the mouth
- Seizures
- Loss of consciousness

---

## First Aid

If poisoning is suspected:

1. Move the person away from the poison if it is safe.
2. Call emergency medical services or a poison control center.
3. Identify the poison if possible.
4. Keep the poison container for medical staff.
5. Follow professional medical advice.

---

## If Poison Is on the Skin

- Remove contaminated clothing.
- Wash the affected area thoroughly with clean water.

---

## If Poison Is in the Eyes

- Rinse the eyes gently with clean water for at least 15 minutes.

---

## Do NOT

Do NOT:

- Force vomiting unless instructed by a healthcare professional.
- Give food or drink unless advised.
- Give home remedies.
- Delay seeking medical help.

---

## Emergency Signs

Call emergency medical services immediately if:

- The person is unconscious.
- Breathing becomes difficult.
- Seizures occur.
- Severe burns develop.
- The poison is highly toxic.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I make the person vomit?

No. Do not induce vomiting unless specifically instructed by a healthcare professional.

### Should I keep the medicine bottle?

Yes. It helps healthcare professionals identify the poison.
