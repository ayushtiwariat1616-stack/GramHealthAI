# Topic: Fracture

ID: FA004

Category: First Aid

---

## Overview

A fracture is a break or crack in a bone. It may result from falls, road traffic accidents, sports injuries, or other trauma. Proper first aid helps reduce pain and prevents further injury until medical treatment is available.

---

## Symptoms

Common signs include:

- Severe pain
- Swelling
- Bruising
- Difficulty moving the limb
- Deformity
- Bone visible through the skin (open fracture)
- Inability to bear weight

---

## First Aid

If a fracture is suspected:

1. Keep the injured person still.
2. Do not try to straighten the bone.
3. Support the injured area using a splint if trained to do so.
4. Apply a cold pack wrapped in a cloth to reduce swelling.
5. Seek medical care immediately.

---

## Open Fracture

If the bone is visible:

- Cover the wound with a clean sterile dressing.
- Do not push the bone back inside.
- Control bleeding by applying gentle pressure around the wound.
- Call emergency medical services.

---

## Do NOT

Do NOT:

- Move the injured limb unnecessarily.
- Attempt to straighten the bone.
- Massage the injured area.
- Allow the person to walk on an injured leg.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Every suspected fracture should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

---

## Emergency Signs

Call emergency medical services immediately if:

- Bone is protruding through the skin.
- Heavy bleeding occurs.
- The person cannot move.
- Neck or back injury is suspected.
- The person loses consciousness.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I try to straighten a broken bone?

No. Keep the limb still and seek medical care.

### Can I walk with a fracture?

Walking may worsen the injury. Avoid putting weight on the affected limb until examined.

---

## Disclaimer

This information is intended for first-aid education only and should not replace emergency medical care.
