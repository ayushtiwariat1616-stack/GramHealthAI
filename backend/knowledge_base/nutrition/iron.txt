# Topic: Iron

ID: NUT005

Category: Nutrition

---

## Overview

Iron is an important mineral that helps the body produce hemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood. Adequate iron intake helps prevent iron deficiency anemia.

Children, adolescents, pregnant women, and women of reproductive age have higher iron requirements.

---

## Benefits of Iron

Iron helps:

- Produce healthy red blood cells
- Carry oxygen throughout the body
- Prevent anemia
- Improve energy levels
- Support brain development

---

## Iron-Rich Foods

Animal sources:

- Liver
- Lean meat
- Fish
- Eggs

Plant sources:

- Spinach
- Green leafy vegetables
- Lentils (Dal)
- Chickpeas
- Rajma
- Soybeans
- Jaggery
- Sesame seeds

---

## Improve Iron Absorption

Vitamin C helps the body absorb iron.

Good sources include:

- Oranges
- Lemon
- Guava
- Tomatoes
- Amla

---

## Signs of Iron Deficiency

- Fatigue
- Weakness
- Pale skin
- Dizziness
- Shortness of breath

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I take iron tablets on my own?

No. Iron supplements should only be taken after medical advice.

### Does vitamin C help?

Yes. Vitamin C improves iron absorption from food.
