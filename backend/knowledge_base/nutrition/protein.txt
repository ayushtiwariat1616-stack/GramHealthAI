# Topic: Protein

ID: NUT004

Category: Nutrition

---

## Overview

Protein is an essential nutrient that helps build and repair muscles, skin, organs, and other body tissues. It also supports the immune system and healthy growth in children.

People of all ages need adequate protein as part of a balanced diet.

---

## Benefits of Protein

Protein helps:

- Build muscles
- Repair body tissues
- Support growth in children
- Maintain healthy skin and hair
- Produce enzymes and hormones
- Strengthen the immune system

---

## Good Sources of Protein

Animal sources:

- Eggs
- Fish
- Chicken
- Lean meat
- Milk
- Curd
- Paneer

Plant sources:

- Dal
- Rajma
- Chana
- Soybeans
- Tofu
- Peanuts
- Nuts and seeds

---

## Daily Tips

- Include a protein-rich food in every meal.
- Eat a variety of protein sources.
- Combine cereals with pulses for better nutrition.

---

## Protein Deficiency

Low protein intake may cause:

- Muscle weakness
- Poor growth in children
- Frequent infections
- Slow wound healing
- Fatigue

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can vegetarians get enough protein?

Yes. Dal, beans, soy products, dairy products, and nuts provide good protein.

### Is eating too much protein harmful?

Most people should consume protein in balanced amounts as part of a healthy diet. Consult a healthcare professional for special dietary needs.
