# Topic: Calcium

ID: NUT006

Category: Nutrition

---

## Overview

Calcium is an essential mineral needed for strong bones and teeth. It also helps muscles, nerves, and the heart function properly.

Adequate calcium intake is important throughout life, especially during childhood, adolescence, pregnancy, and older age.

---

## Benefits of Calcium

Calcium helps:

- Build strong bones
- Maintain healthy teeth
- Support muscle movement
- Help normal nerve function
- Support heart function

---

## Calcium-Rich Foods

Good sources include:

- Milk
- Curd
- Paneer
- Cheese
- Ragi
- Sesame seeds
- Almonds
- Soy products
- Green leafy vegetables

---

## Vitamin D

Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium.

Sources include:

- Safe sunlight exposure
- Fortified foods
- Foods recommended by healthcare professionals

---

## Calcium Deficiency

Low calcium intake may contribute to:

- Weak bones
- Muscle cramps
- Bone pain
- Increased risk of fractures

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is milk the only source of calcium?

No. Many plant foods also contain calcium.

### Should I take calcium supplements?

Only if recommended by a healthcare professional.
