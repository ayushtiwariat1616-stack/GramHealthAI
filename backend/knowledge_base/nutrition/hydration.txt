# Topic: Hydration

ID: NUT002

Category: Nutrition

---

## Overview

Hydration means providing the body with enough fluids to perform normal functions. Water is essential for digestion, circulation, temperature regulation, joint lubrication, and waste removal.

Both children and adults should drink enough safe, clean water throughout the day.

---

## Signs of Good Hydration

A well-hydrated person usually has:

- Light yellow urine
- Moist lips and mouth
- Normal energy levels
- Good concentration
- Normal body temperature

---

## Signs of Dehydration

Common signs include:

- Thirst
- Dry mouth
- Dark yellow urine
- Dizziness
- Headache
- Fatigue
- Dry skin

Severe dehydration may cause confusion, rapid heartbeat, and fainting.

---

## Daily Hydration Tips

- Drink water regularly throughout the day.
- Drink more during hot weather.
- Increase fluid intake during illness with fever or diarrhea.
- Carry a water bottle while travelling.
- Drink safe, clean, or boiled water.

---

## Who Needs Extra Fluids?

Additional fluids may be needed by:

- Children
- Older adults
- Pregnant women
- Breastfeeding mothers
- People working outdoors
- People exercising
- Individuals with fever, vomiting, or diarrhea

---

## Preventing Dehydration

- Drink water before feeling very thirsty.
- Eat fruits with high water content.
- Avoid excessive sugary drinks.
- Replace lost fluids during illness using ORS if advised.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Seek medical advice if:

- Vomiting continues.
- Diarrhea lasts more than one day in children or several days in adults.
- The person cannot drink fluids.
- Signs of severe dehydration develop.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical attention if there is:

- Confusion
- Very little urine
- Loss of consciousness
- Extreme weakness
- Sunken eyes in children
- Persistent vomiting

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can tea or coffee replace water?

Water should remain the main source of hydration. Tea and coffee do not completely replace water.

### Is drinking too much water harmful?

Excessive water intake in a short period may be harmful. Drink according to your body's needs and medical advice.
