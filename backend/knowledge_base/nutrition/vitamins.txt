# Topic: Vitamins and Minerals

ID: NUT004

Category: Nutrition

---

## Overview

Vitamins and minerals are essential nutrients required in small amounts for normal growth, immunity, bone health, energy production, and proper functioning of the body.

Eating a varied and balanced diet usually provides all the nutrients most healthy people need.

---

## Important Vitamins

### Vitamin A

Functions:

- Supports vision
- Strengthens immunity
- Maintains healthy skin

Sources:

- Carrots
- Sweet potato
- Mango
- Spinach
- Milk
- Eggs

---

### Vitamin B Complex

Functions:

- Energy production
- Healthy nerves
- Red blood cell formation

Sources:

- Whole grains
- Milk
- Eggs
- Pulses
- Meat

---

### Vitamin C

Functions:

- Improves immunity
- Helps wound healing
- Increases iron absorption

Sources:

- Orange
- Lemon
- Guava
- Amla
- Tomatoes

---

### Vitamin D

Functions:

- Strong bones
- Calcium absorption

Sources:

- Sunlight
- Eggs
- Fish
- Fortified milk

---

### Vitamin E

Functions:

- Protects body cells
- Supports immunity

Sources:

- Almonds
- Sunflower seeds
- Vegetable oils

---

### Vitamin K

Functions:

- Blood clotting
- Bone health

Sources:

- Green leafy vegetables

## Signs of Deficiency

Possible symptoms include:

- Fatigue
- Weakness
- Poor immunity
- Bone pain
- Vision problems
- Hair loss
- Slow wound healing

---

## Prevention

Maintain good nutrition by:

- Eating colorful fruits and vegetables.
- Including dairy products.
- Eating pulses regularly.
- Drinking enough water.
- Spending time in sunlight safely.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Seek medical advice if:

- Fatigue persists.
- Bone pain develops.
- Frequent infections occur.
- Poor growth is noticed in children.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should everyone take vitamin supplements?

No. Most healthy people can obtain enough vitamins from a balanced diet. Supplements should only be taken on medical advice.

### Can too many vitamins be harmful?

Yes. Excessive intake of certain vitamins or minerals may cause health problems.
