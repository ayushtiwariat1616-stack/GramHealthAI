# Topic: Pregnancy Care

ID: MCH001

Category: Maternal & Child Health

---

## Overview

Pregnancy care, also called antenatal care (ANC), helps protect the health of both the mother and the developing baby. Regular check-ups allow healthcare professionals to monitor the pregnancy, identify possible problems early, and provide guidance for a healthy pregnancy.

Every pregnant woman should receive regular antenatal care from a qualified healthcare provider.

---

## Importance of Antenatal Care

Regular pregnancy check-ups help:

- Monitor the baby's growth.
- Detect complications early.
- Monitor the mother's health.
- Provide vaccinations.
- Provide iron and folic acid supplements.
- Offer nutrition and breastfeeding advice.

---

## Healthy Pregnancy Tips

Pregnant women should:

- Eat a balanced diet.
- Drink plenty of clean water.
- Get enough rest.
- Stay physically active as advised by a healthcare professional.
- Take prescribed iron and folic acid tablets.
- Attend all scheduled antenatal visits.
- Sleep adequately.

---

## Foods to Eat

Choose nutritious foods such as:

- Whole grains
- Rice and wheat
- Dal and pulses
- Milk and dairy products
- Eggs
- Green leafy vegetables
- Fruits
- Nuts and seeds

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- Alcohol
- Tobacco
- Smoking
- Illegal drugs
- Raw or undercooked meat
- Unpasteurized milk
- Unsafe drinking water
- Medicines without medical advice

---

## Recommended Vaccinations

Healthcare professionals may recommend vaccines such as:

- Tetanus-containing vaccines
- Other vaccines according to national guidelines

Always follow your healthcare provider's advice.

---

## Danger Signs

Seek immediate medical care if there is:

- Heavy vaginal bleeding
- Severe abdominal pain
- Severe headache
- Blurred vision
- Swelling of the face or hands
- High fever
- Reduced fetal movement (later in pregnancy)
- Convulsions

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many antenatal visits are recommended?

Pregnant women should attend all antenatal visits recommended by their healthcare provider.

### Should I take medicines during pregnancy?

Take only medicines prescribed or approved by a qualified healthcare professional.
