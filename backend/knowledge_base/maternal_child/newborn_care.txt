# Topic: Newborn Care

ID: MCH002

Category: Maternal & Child Health

---

## Overview

The first 28 days after birth are called the newborn period. Proper newborn care is essential for healthy growth, preventing infections, and reducing the risk of serious illness.

Parents and caregivers should provide warmth, proper feeding, hygiene, and timely medical care.

---

## Essential Newborn Care

Provide:

- Immediate breastfeeding.
- Skin-to-skin contact after birth.
- Warm clothing.
- A clean sleeping environment.
- Proper cord care.
- Timely vaccinations.
- Regular health check-ups.

---

## Umbilical Cord Care

Keep the umbilical cord:

- Clean.
- Dry.
- Exposed to air when possible.

Do not apply:

- Oil
- Powder
- Ash
- Turmeric
- Herbal products

Allow the cord to fall off naturally.

---

## Safe Sleep

To reduce the risk of sleep-related accidents:

- Place the baby on the back to sleep.
- Use a firm mattress.
- Avoid pillows.
- Keep soft toys away from the sleeping area.
- Avoid overheating.

---

## Bathing

- Delay the first bath according to healthcare guidance.
- Use warm water.
- Dry the baby immediately.
- Keep the baby warm after bathing.

---

## Hygiene

Always:

- Wash hands before touching the baby.
- Keep feeding items clean.
- Maintain a clean home environment.

---

## Warning Signs

Seek medical care immediately if the baby has:

- Difficulty breathing
- Poor feeding
- Fever
- Low body temperature
- Yellow skin soon after birth or worsening jaundice
- Convulsions
- Persistent vomiting
- Extreme sleepiness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should a newborn feed?

Most newborns feed frequently, often every 2–3 hours, though feeding patterns may vary.

### Should I apply anything to the umbilical cord?

No. Keep it clean and dry unless advised otherwise by a healthcare professional.
