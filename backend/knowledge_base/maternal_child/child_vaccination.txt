# Topic: Child Vaccination

ID: MCH004

Category: Maternal & Child Health

---

## Overview

Vaccination protects children from serious infectious diseases by helping the immune system recognize and fight harmful germs.

Children should receive vaccines according to the National Immunization Schedule recommended by the Government of India.

---

## Why Vaccination Is Important

Vaccination helps:

- Prevent life-threatening diseases.
- Reduce hospital admissions.
- Protect children during early life.
- Prevent outbreaks in the community.

---

## Diseases Prevented

Childhood vaccines help prevent diseases such as:

- Tuberculosis
- Polio
- Hepatitis B
- Measles
- Diphtheria
- Pertussis (Whooping Cough)
- Tetanus
- Rotavirus infection
- Pneumococcal disease

---

## Vaccination Schedule

Parents should:

- Follow the national immunization schedule.
- Attend every vaccination appointment.
- Keep the child's vaccination card safely.

---

## Common Side Effects

Some children may experience:

- Mild fever
- Pain at the injection site
- Swelling
- Irritability
- Sleepiness

These effects usually improve within a few days.

---

## When to Seek Medical Care

Seek immediate medical attention if the child develops:

- Difficulty breathing
- Severe allergic reaction
- Persistent high fever
- Convulsions
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should a child miss vaccinations because of a mild cold?

Usually no. A mild illness is not always a reason to delay vaccination. Follow your healthcare provider's advice.

### Is it important to complete every vaccine dose?

Yes. Completing the recommended schedule provides the best protection.
