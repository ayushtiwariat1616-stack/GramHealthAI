# Topic: Tuberculosis (TB)

ID: DIS005

Category: Infectious Disease

---

## Overview

Tuberculosis (TB) is an infectious disease caused by the bacterium *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*. It mainly affects the lungs but can also affect the brain, kidneys, spine, and other organs. TB is curable with proper treatment, but completing the full course of medicines is essential.

---

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Persistent cough lasting more than two weeks
- Chest pain
- Coughing up blood or sputum
- Fever
- Night sweats
- Weight loss
- Loss of appetite
- Fatigue

---

## Causes

TB is caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*.

The infection spreads through:

- Tiny droplets released into the air when a person with active TB coughs, sneezes, laughs, or speaks.

TB does NOT usually spread through:

- Handshakes
- Sharing food
- Sharing utensils
- Touching surfaces

---

## Risk Factors

The risk is higher in people who:

- Have weak immune systems.
- Have HIV infection.
- Have diabetes.
- Smoke tobacco.
- Live in crowded environments.
- Have poor nutrition.

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Covering the mouth while coughing or sneezing.
- Keeping rooms well ventilated.
- Wearing masks when recommended.
- Early diagnosis and treatment.
- BCG vaccination for infants as recommended.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may recommend:

- Chest X-ray
- Sputum examination
- GeneXpert test
- TB skin test
- Blood tests

---

## Treatment

Treatment usually includes:

- A combination of anti-TB medicines.
- Completing the full treatment course.
- Regular follow-up with healthcare providers.

Do not stop medicines early even if symptoms improve.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Cough lasts more than two weeks.
- Blood appears in sputum.
- Fever and weight loss continue.
- Persistent chest pain develops.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical attention if there is:

- Severe difficulty breathing
- Heavy coughing of blood
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe chest pain

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is TB curable?

Yes. TB is curable with proper treatment.

### Can TB spread to family members?

Yes. Active pulmonary TB can spread through the air.
