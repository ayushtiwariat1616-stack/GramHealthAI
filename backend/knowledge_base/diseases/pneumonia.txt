# Topic: Pneumonia

ID: DIS010

Category: Infectious Disease

---

## Overview

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi. Pneumonia may range from mild to life-threatening, especially in young children, older adults, and people with weakened immune systems.

---

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Fever
- Cough
- Cough with mucus
- Chest pain while breathing or coughing
- Difficulty breathing
- Chills
- Fatigue
- Fast breathing

Older adults may sometimes develop confusion instead of fever.

---

## Causes

Pneumonia may be caused by:

- Bacteria
- Viruses
- Fungi

The infection spreads through respiratory droplets from infected individuals.

---

## Risk Factors

Higher risk includes:

- Children under five years.
- Adults over sixty-five years.
- Smokers.
- People with asthma.
- Diabetes.
- Heart disease.
- Weak immune systems.

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Washing hands regularly.
- Covering coughs and sneezes.
- Avoiding smoking.
- Maintaining good nutrition.
- Getting recommended vaccinations.
- Seeking early treatment for respiratory infections.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may recommend:

- Physical examination.
- Chest X-ray.
- Blood tests.
- Sputum examination.
- Pulse oximetry.

---

## Treatment

Treatment depends on the cause.

It may include:

- Antibiotics for bacterial pneumonia.
- Antiviral medicines in selected viral infections.
- Fever medicines as prescribed.
- Adequate rest.
- Drinking enough fluids.
- Oxygen therapy for severe illness.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Fever is high.
- Cough lasts several days.
- Difficulty breathing develops.
- Chest pain occurs.
- Symptoms become worse.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical care if there is:

- Severe difficulty breathing
- Blue lips or face
- Confusion
- Persistent chest pain
- Oxygen levels become very low
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is pneumonia contagious?

Some forms of pneumonia caused by bacteria or viruses can spread between people.

### Can pneumonia be prevented?

Yes. Vaccination, hand hygiene, avoiding smoking, and early treatment of infections reduce the risk.
