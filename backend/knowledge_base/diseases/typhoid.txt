# Topic: Typhoid Fever

ID: DIS003

Category: Infectious Disease

---

## Overview

Typhoid fever is a bacterial infection caused by *Salmonella Typhi*. It spreads through contaminated food or drinking water and is more common in areas with poor sanitation. Early diagnosis and appropriate antibiotic treatment usually lead to complete recovery.

---

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Persistent high fever
- Headache
- Weakness and fatigue
- Stomach pain
- Loss of appetite
- Constipation or diarrhea
- Dry cough
- Muscle aches

Symptoms usually develop 6 to 30 days after exposure.

---

## Causes

Typhoid fever is caused by the bacterium *Salmonella Typhi*.

The infection spreads through:

- Contaminated food
- Contaminated drinking water
- Poor hand hygiene
- Close contact with an infected person or carrier

Typhoid does NOT spread through the air.

---

## Risk Factors

Risk increases if a person:

- Drinks unsafe water.
- Eats unhygienic street food.
- Lives in areas with poor sanitation.
- Travels to regions where typhoid is common.

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Drinking clean and safe water.
- Washing hands before eating and after using the toilet.
- Eating freshly cooked food.
- Washing fruits and vegetables thoroughly.
- Getting vaccinated if recommended.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may recommend:

- Blood culture
- Stool culture
- Urine culture
- Complete blood count (CBC)

---

## Treatment

Treatment usually includes:

- Antibiotics prescribed by a doctor.
- Drinking plenty of fluids.
- Adequate rest.
- Completing the full course of medicines.

Do not stop antibiotics early even if symptoms improve.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Fever lasts more than 2–3 days.
- Severe abdominal pain develops.
- Continuous vomiting occurs.
- The patient becomes confused or extremely weak.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical care if there is:

- Severe abdominal swelling
- Bloody stools
- Persistent vomiting
- Difficulty staying awake
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is typhoid contagious?

Yes. It spreads through contaminated food and water.

### Can typhoid be prevented?

Yes. Good hygiene, safe drinking water, proper sanitation and vaccination greatly reduce the risk.
