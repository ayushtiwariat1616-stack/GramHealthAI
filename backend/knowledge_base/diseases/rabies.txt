# Topic: Rabies

ID: DIS009

Category: Infectious Disease

---

## Overview

Rabies is a deadly viral disease that affects the brain and nervous system. It spreads through the bite or scratch of an infected animal, most commonly dogs. Rabies is almost always fatal once symptoms appear, but it is completely preventable with immediate medical treatment after exposure.

---

## Symptoms

Early symptoms include:

- Fever
- Headache
- Weakness
- Pain or tingling at the bite site

Later symptoms include:

- Difficulty swallowing
- Fear of water (hydrophobia)
- Excessive saliva
- Anxiety
- Confusion
- Hallucinations
- Muscle spasms
- Paralysis

---

## Causes

Rabies is caused by the rabies virus.

It spreads through:

- Bite of an infected animal.
- Scratch from an infected animal.
- Saliva entering an open wound or eyes, nose, or mouth.

Common animals include:

- Dogs
- Bats
- Cats
- Foxes
- Monkeys

---

## Risk Factors

Higher risk includes:

- Animal bites.
- Working with animals.
- Traveling to areas where rabies is common.
- Delayed medical treatment after an animal bite.

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Vaccinating pets.
- Avoiding contact with stray animals.
- Washing animal bite wounds immediately with soap and running water for at least 15 minutes.
- Seeking medical care immediately after any animal bite.
- Taking rabies vaccination if recommended by a healthcare professional.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may evaluate:

- Bite history.
- Physical examination.
- Laboratory tests (mainly after symptoms appear).

Treatment should begin immediately after exposure without waiting for symptoms.

---

## Treatment

After an animal bite:

- Wash the wound immediately.
- Visit the nearest healthcare facility.
- Receive rabies vaccine if advised.
- Receive Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG) when indicated.

Once symptoms appear, survival is extremely rare.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Immediately after:

- Any dog bite.
- Cat bite.
- Monkey bite.
- Bat bite.
- Animal scratch that breaks the skin.

Do NOT wait for symptoms.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek emergency care immediately if:

- Difficulty swallowing develops.
- Confusion appears.
- Muscle spasms occur.
- Fear of water develops.
- Difficulty breathing develops.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should every dog bite be treated?

Yes. Every animal bite should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

### Can rabies be cured after symptoms appear?

Rabies is almost always fatal after symptoms begin. Early treatment after exposure is essential.
