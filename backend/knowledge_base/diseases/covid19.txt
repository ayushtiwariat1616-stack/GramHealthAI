# Topic: COVID-19

ID: DIS006

Category: Infectious Disease

---

## Overview

COVID-19 is a viral respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It spreads mainly through respiratory droplets and close contact with infected individuals. Most people recover with supportive care, but older adults and people with underlying health conditions may develop severe illness.

---

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Fever
- Cough
- Sore throat
- Runny nose
- Fatigue
- Headache
- Body aches
- Loss of taste or smell
- Difficulty breathing in severe cases

Symptoms usually appear 2 to 14 days after exposure.

---

## Causes

COVID-19 is caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus.

The infection spreads through:

- Respiratory droplets.
- Close contact with infected individuals.
- Poorly ventilated indoor spaces.

---

## Risk Factors

Higher risk of severe illness includes:

- Older adults.
- People with diabetes.
- Heart disease.
- Chronic lung disease.
- Kidney disease.
- Weak immune systems.

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Washing hands regularly.
- Covering coughs and sneezes.
- Staying home when sick.
- Improving indoor ventilation.
- Following current public health recommendations.
- Keeping vaccinations up to date if advised.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may recommend:

- RT-PCR test
- Rapid antigen test
- Physical examination
- Chest imaging when required

---

## Treatment

Treatment depends on severity.

It may include:

- Rest.
- Drinking plenty of fluids.
- Medicines prescribed by a healthcare professional.
- Oxygen therapy or hospital care for severe illness.

Do not take medicines without medical advice.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Fever persists.
- Symptoms worsen.
- Difficulty breathing develops.
- Oxygen levels decrease.
- Severe weakness occurs.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek emergency medical attention immediately if there is:

- Severe difficulty breathing
- Chest pain
- Confusion
- Blue lips or face
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can COVID-19 be prevented?

Yes. Good hygiene, ventilation, vaccination (when recommended), and avoiding exposure to infected individuals help reduce risk.

### Does everyone need hospitalization?

No. Many people recover at home, while severe cases require hospital treatment.
