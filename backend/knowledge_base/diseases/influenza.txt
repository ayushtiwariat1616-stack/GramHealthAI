# Topic: Influenza (Seasonal Flu)

ID: DIS007

Category: Infectious Disease

---

## Overview

Influenza, commonly called the flu, is a contagious viral infection that affects the nose, throat, and lungs. It is caused by influenza viruses and spreads easily from person to person. Most people recover within a week, but some individuals, especially older adults, young children, pregnant women, and people with chronic illnesses, may develop serious complications.

---

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Sudden fever
- Chills
- Cough
- Sore throat
- Runny or blocked nose
- Headache
- Muscle aches
- Fatigue
- Weakness

Some people may also experience vomiting or diarrhea, especially children.

---

## Causes

Influenza is caused by influenza viruses, mainly:

- Influenza A
- Influenza B

The virus spreads through:

- Respiratory droplets from coughing or sneezing.
- Close contact with infected individuals.
- Touching contaminated surfaces and then touching the eyes, nose, or mouth.

---

## Risk Factors

Higher risk includes:

- Children under 5 years.
- Adults over 65 years.
- Pregnant women.
- People with asthma.
- Diabetes.
- Heart disease.
- Weak immune systems.

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Washing hands regularly.
- Covering your mouth while coughing or sneezing.
- Staying home when sick.
- Keeping rooms well ventilated.
- Avoiding close contact with sick people.
- Getting the seasonal influenza vaccine if recommended.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may recommend:

- Physical examination.
- Rapid influenza diagnostic test.
- RT-PCR test when necessary.

---

## Treatment

Treatment usually includes:

- Plenty of rest.
- Drinking enough fluids.
- Medicines prescribed by a healthcare professional.
- Antiviral medicines for some patients when started early.

Do not take antibiotics unless prescribed, as antibiotics do not treat viral infections.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Fever lasts more than three days.
- Symptoms become worse.
- Difficulty breathing develops.
- Chest pain occurs.
- A child or older adult becomes unusually weak.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical care if there is:

- Difficulty breathing
- Chest pain
- Blue lips or face
- Confusion
- Seizures
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is influenza the same as the common cold?

No. Influenza usually causes more severe symptoms than the common cold.

### Can influenza be prevented?

Yes. Good hygiene and seasonal vaccination help reduce the risk.
