# Topic: Anemia

ID: DIS013

Category: Non-Communicable Disease

---

## Overview

Anemia is a condition in which the body does not have enough healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin to carry oxygen to the body's tissues. Iron deficiency is the most common cause, but anemia can also result from vitamin deficiencies, blood loss, chronic diseases, or inherited disorders.

Women, children, and pregnant women are at higher risk.

---

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Fatigue
- Weakness
- Pale skin
- Dizziness
- Headache
- Shortness of breath
- Fast heartbeat
- Cold hands and feet
- Poor concentration

Some people with mild anemia may have no symptoms.

---

## Causes

Anemia may result from:

- Iron deficiency
- Vitamin B12 deficiency
- Folate deficiency
- Blood loss
- Chronic diseases
- Inherited blood disorders

---

## Prevention

Reduce the risk by:

- Eating iron-rich foods.
- Including vitamin C-rich foods with meals.
- Taking iron supplements only if prescribed.
- Regular health check-ups during pregnancy.
- Treating underlying medical conditions.

---

## Diagnosis

Doctors may recommend:

- Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- Hemoglobin test
- Iron studies
- Vitamin B12 test
- Folate test

---

## Treatment

Treatment depends on the cause and may include:

- Iron supplements
- Vitamin supplements
- Dietary changes
- Medicines for underlying diseases

Do not self-medicate with iron tablets.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Fatigue persists.
- You develop pale skin.
- You experience dizziness or breathlessness.
- Symptoms interfere with daily activities.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek emergency medical care if there is:

- Severe shortness of breath
- Chest pain
- Fainting
- Rapid heartbeat
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is anemia curable?

Most cases improve with proper diagnosis, treatment, and nutrition.

### Can diet alone cure anemia?

Some mild cases improve with diet, but many require medical evaluation and treatment.
