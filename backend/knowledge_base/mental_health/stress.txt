# Topic: Stress

ID: MH001

Category: Mental Health

---

## Overview

Stress is the body's natural response to challenges or difficult situations. A small amount of stress can help people stay focused, but long-term or severe stress can affect both physical and mental health.

Everyone experiences stress at different times in life.

---

## Common Causes

Stress may be caused by:

- Work or studies
- Financial problems
- Family issues
- Illness
- Major life changes
- Lack of sleep

---

## Symptoms

Stress may cause:

### Physical symptoms

- Headache
- Muscle tension
- Fatigue
- Poor sleep
- Fast heartbeat
- Upset stomach

### Emotional symptoms

- Irritability
- Worry
- Feeling overwhelmed
- Difficulty concentrating
- Mood changes

---

## Managing Stress

Healthy ways to manage stress include:

- Getting enough sleep
- Regular physical activity
- Deep breathing exercises
- Talking to trusted family or friends
- Taking short breaks during work
- Spending time on enjoyable activities

---

## Avoid

Avoid using:

- Alcohol
- Tobacco
- Drugs

These may worsen stress over time.

---

## When to Seek Help

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Stress lasts for several weeks.
- It interferes with daily life.
- You cannot manage it on your own.
- It affects your work, studies, or relationships.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate help if stress is accompanied by:

- Thoughts of self-harm
- Thoughts of suicide
- Extreme panic
- Loss of touch with reality

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is stress normal?

Yes. Everyone experiences stress, but prolonged stress should be addressed.

### Can exercise help?

Yes. Regular physical activity can improve mood and reduce stress.
