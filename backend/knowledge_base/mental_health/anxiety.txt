# Topic: Anxiety

ID: MH002

Category: Mental Health

---

## Overview

Anxiety is a feeling of fear, worry, or nervousness. Occasional anxiety is a normal part of life, but persistent or excessive anxiety may indicate an anxiety disorder that requires professional care.

---

## Common Symptoms

People with anxiety may experience:

- Excessive worrying
- Restlessness
- Feeling nervous
- Rapid heartbeat
- Sweating
- Trembling
- Difficulty concentrating
- Trouble sleeping
- Shortness of breath

---

## Common Triggers

Anxiety may be triggered by:

- Stress
- Financial problems
- Exams
- Work pressure
- Family issues
- Health concerns

---

## Self-Care Tips

Helpful strategies include:

- Deep breathing exercises
- Regular physical activity
- Healthy sleep habits
- Limiting caffeine if it worsens symptoms
- Talking with trusted friends or family
- Relaxation techniques such as meditation

---

## When to Seek Help

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Anxiety continues for several weeks.
- It interferes with daily activities.
- Panic attacks occur frequently.
- Self-care measures are not helping.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical help if a person has:

- Thoughts of self-harm
- Thoughts of suicide
- Severe panic with difficulty breathing
- Loss of consciousness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is anxiety the same as stress?

No. Stress is usually a response to a specific situation, while anxiety may continue even when there is no immediate danger.

### Can anxiety be treated?

Yes. Many people improve with counseling, lifestyle changes, and when appropriate, medical treatment.
