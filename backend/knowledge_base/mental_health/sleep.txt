# Topic: Healthy Sleep

ID: MH004

Category: Mental Health

---

## Overview

Good sleep is essential for physical health, mental well-being, learning, memory, and overall quality of life. Adults generally need about 7–9 hours of sleep each night, though sleep needs vary with age.

Poor sleep over a long period can affect health.

---

## Benefits of Good Sleep

Healthy sleep helps:

- Improve memory
- Increase concentration
- Strengthen the immune system
- Improve mood
- Support heart health
- Maintain healthy body weight

---

## Causes of Poor Sleep

Sleep problems may occur due to:

- Stress
- Anxiety
- Depression
- Too much caffeine
- Excessive screen time before bed
- Irregular sleep schedule
- Certain medical conditions

---

## Healthy Sleep Habits

Improve sleep by:

- Going to bed at the same time every day.
- Waking up at a regular time.
- Avoiding large meals before bedtime.
- Limiting caffeine in the evening.
- Reducing screen use before sleep.
- Keeping the bedroom quiet, dark, and comfortable.
- Exercising regularly during the day.

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- Smoking before bedtime.
- Alcohol as a sleep aid.
- Long daytime naps.
- Heavy exercise immediately before sleeping.

---

## When to Seek Medical Advice

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Sleep problems continue for several weeks.
- Loud snoring is associated with pauses in breathing.
- Daytime sleepiness interferes with daily life.
- Sleep problems affect work or school performance.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical attention if:

- A person becomes difficult to wake.
- Confusion follows a sleep-related event.
- Breathing repeatedly stops during sleep and causes severe symptoms.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many hours should adults sleep?

Most healthy adults need about 7–9 hours of sleep each night.

### Can using a phone before bed affect sleep?

Yes. Bright screens may make it harder to fall asleep.
