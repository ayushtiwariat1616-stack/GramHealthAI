# Topic: Depression

ID: MH003

Category: Mental Health

---

## Overview

Depression is a common mental health condition that affects how a person feels, thinks, and behaves. It is more than feeling sad for a few days. Depression can interfere with work, studies, relationships, and daily life, but it is treatable.

---

## Common Symptoms

A person with depression may experience:

- Persistent sadness
- Loss of interest in activities
- Feeling hopeless
- Low energy
- Difficulty concentrating
- Changes in appetite
- Changes in sleep
- Feelings of worthlessness
- Irritability

Symptoms usually last for at least two weeks.

---

## Possible Causes

Depression may be related to:

- Stressful life events
- Family history
- Chronic illness
- Financial difficulties
- Social isolation
- Traumatic experiences

---

## Self-Care

Helpful habits include:

- Maintaining a daily routine.
- Regular physical activity.
- Eating a balanced diet.
- Sleeping regularly.
- Spending time with supportive family and friends.
- Talking openly about your feelings.

---

## Treatment

Treatment may include:

- Counseling or psychotherapy.
- Medicines prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional.
- Lifestyle changes.
- Family and social support.

Many people recover with proper treatment.

---

## When to Seek Help

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Symptoms last more than two weeks.
- Daily activities become difficult.
- Feelings continue to worsen.
- Self-care is not helping.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate emergency medical help if a person:

- Has thoughts of self-harm.
- Talks about suicide.
- Attempts self-harm.
- Cannot care for themselves.
- Shows sudden extreme changes in behaviour.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can depression be treated?

Yes. Depression is treatable, and many people recover with appropriate care.

### Is depression a sign of weakness?

No. Depression is a medical condition and is not caused by personal weakness.
