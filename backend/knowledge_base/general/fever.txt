# Topic: Fever

ID: GEN001

Category: General Symptoms

---

## Overview

Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually caused by the body's response to an infection or illness. It is a symptom rather than a disease itself.

A normal body temperature is around **37°C (98.6°F)**, though it may vary slightly from person to person.

---

## Common Causes

Fever may occur due to:

- Viral infections
- Bacterial infections
- Influenza (Flu)
- COVID-19
- Malaria
- Dengue
- Typhoid
- Urinary tract infections
- Vaccination (temporary mild fever)

---

## Common Symptoms

A person with fever may have:

- Increased body temperature
- Chills
- Sweating
- Headache
- Muscle aches
- Weakness
- Loss of appetite
- Dehydration

---

## Home Care

For mild fever:

- Drink plenty of clean water.
- Get enough rest.
- Wear light clothing.
- Eat light, nutritious meals.
- Monitor body temperature regularly.
- Take medicines only as advised by a healthcare professional.

---

## Preventing Dehydration

While having a fever:

- Drink water regularly.
- Use Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) if advised.
- Drink soups or other fluids if tolerated.

---

## Do NOT

Do NOT:

- Take antibiotics without a doctor's advice.
- Cover yourself with heavy blankets if the body is already hot.
- Ignore a persistent high fever.
- Give medicines in incorrect doses.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Seek medical care if:

- Fever lasts more than 2–3 days.
- Temperature is very high.
- Fever returns repeatedly.
- Severe headache develops.
- Rash appears.
- Persistent vomiting occurs.
- There is difficulty breathing.

---

## Emergency Signs

Call emergency medical services immediately if fever is accompanied by:

- Confusion
- Seizures
- Difficulty breathing
- Unconsciousness
- Severe neck stiffness

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is every fever dangerous?

No. Many fevers are caused by common infections and improve with proper care, but persistent or very high fever requires medical evaluation.

### Should I stay hydrated?

Yes. Drinking enough fluids is very important during fever.
