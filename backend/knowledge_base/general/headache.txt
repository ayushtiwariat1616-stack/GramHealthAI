# Topic: Headache

ID: GEN002

Category: General Symptoms

---

## Overview

A headache is pain or discomfort in the head, scalp, or neck. Most headaches are not serious and improve with rest, hydration, or appropriate treatment. However, some headaches can indicate a medical emergency.

---

## Common Causes

Headaches may be caused by:

- Stress
- Lack of sleep
- Dehydration
- Fever
- Eye strain
- Migraine
- Sinus infection
- High blood pressure
- Certain illnesses

---

## Common Symptoms

Symptoms may include:

- Pain on one or both sides of the head
- Pressure around the forehead
- Throbbing pain
- Sensitivity to light or sound
- Nausea (especially with migraine)

---

## Home Care

For mild headaches:

- Rest in a quiet room.
- Drink enough water.
- Eat regular meals.
- Get adequate sleep.
- Reduce screen time if eye strain is suspected.
- Take medicines only as advised by a healthcare professional.

---

## Prevention

You can reduce headaches by:

- Sleeping regularly.
- Staying hydrated.
- Managing stress.
- Exercising regularly.
- Avoiding skipping meals.

---

## Do NOT

Do NOT:

- Take painkillers repeatedly without medical advice.
- Ignore severe or sudden headaches.
- Continue strenuous activity if symptoms worsen.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Headaches occur frequently.
- Pain lasts several days.
- Medicines do not help.
- Headaches interfere with daily life.

---

## Emergency Signs

Seek immediate medical care if headache is accompanied by:

- Sudden severe pain ("worst headache ever")
- Loss of consciousness
- Weakness on one side
- Difficulty speaking
- Vision changes
- High fever with stiff neck
- Seizures

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can dehydration cause headaches?

Yes. Not drinking enough water is a common cause of headaches.

### When should I worry about a headache?

Seek medical attention if it is sudden, severe, or associated with neurological symptoms.
