# Topic: Cough

ID: GEN003

Category: General Symptoms

---

## Overview

A cough is the body's natural reflex to clear the airways of mucus, dust, or irritants. Most coughs are caused by viral infections and improve within a few weeks.

A persistent or severe cough may require medical evaluation.

---

## Common Causes

Cough may occur due to:

- Common cold
- Influenza (Flu)
- COVID-19
- Asthma
- Allergies
- Pneumonia
- Tuberculosis
- Smoking
- Air pollution

---

## Types of Cough

- Dry cough (no mucus)
- Wet cough (with mucus)
- Persistent cough (lasting several weeks)

---

## Home Care

For mild cough:

- Drink warm fluids.
- Stay hydrated.
- Rest adequately.
- Avoid smoking and second-hand smoke.
- Follow medical advice before taking cough medicines.

---

## Do NOT

Do NOT:

- Smoke.
- Self-medicate with antibiotics.
- Ignore a cough lasting more than three weeks.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Cough lasts more than three weeks.
- High fever develops.
- Thick or bloody mucus is present.
- Chest pain occurs.
- Breathing becomes difficult.

---

## Emergency Signs

Call emergency medical services immediately if:

- Severe difficulty breathing develops.
- Lips become blue.
- The person coughs up a large amount of blood.
- The person becomes unconscious.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a cough be caused by allergies?

Yes. Allergies commonly cause a dry cough.

### Should antibiotics be taken for every cough?

No. Many coughs are caused by viruses and do not require antibiotics.
