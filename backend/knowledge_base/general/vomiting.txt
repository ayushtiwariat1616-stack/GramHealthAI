# Topic: Vomiting

ID: GEN005

Category: General Symptoms

---

## Overview

Vomiting is the forceful emptying of stomach contents through the mouth. It may occur due to infections, food poisoning, motion sickness, pregnancy, or other illnesses.

Repeated vomiting can lead to dehydration.

---

## Common Causes

- Viral infections
- Food poisoning
- Motion sickness
- Pregnancy
- Migraine
- Certain medicines

---

## Symptoms

- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Stomach discomfort
- Weakness
- Loss of appetite
- Dehydration

---

## Home Care

- Sip small amounts of clean water.
- Use ORS if advised.
- Eat bland foods after vomiting stops.
- Rest.
- Avoid oily and spicy foods.

---

## Do NOT

Do NOT:

- Eat heavy meals immediately.
- Ignore repeated vomiting.
- Take medicines without medical advice.

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Consult a healthcare professional if:

- Vomiting lasts more than 24 hours.
- Blood appears in vomit.
- Severe stomach pain develops.
- Signs of dehydration appear.

---

## Emergency Signs

Call emergency medical services immediately if:

- The person becomes unconscious.
- Severe dehydration develops.
- Vomiting follows a serious head injury.
- Vomiting is accompanied by severe chest pain.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can vomiting cause dehydration?

Yes. Drinking fluids gradually helps replace lost water.

### When can I eat again?

Start with light foods after vomiting has stopped and fluids are tolerated.
