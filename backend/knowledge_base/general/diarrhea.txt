# Topic: Diarrhea

ID: GEN004

Category: General Symptoms

---

## Overview

Diarrhea is the passage of loose or watery stools several times a day. It is commonly caused by infections, contaminated food or water, or certain medicines.

Most cases improve within a few days, but dehydration can become serious.

---

## Common Causes

- Viral infections
- Bacterial infections
- Contaminated food
- Unsafe drinking water
- Food poisoning
- Certain medicines

---

## Symptoms

- Loose stools
- Stomach cramps
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Fever
- Weakness
- Dehydration

---

## Home Care

- Drink plenty of clean water.
- Use Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS).
- Continue eating light meals.
- Wash hands regularly.
- Rest adequately.

---

## Foods to Eat

Choose:

- Rice
- Bananas
- Toast
- Khichdi
- Soup
- Curd (if tolerated)

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- Oily foods
- Spicy foods
- Alcohol
- Sugary drinks
- Unsafe water

---

## When to Visit a Doctor

Seek medical care if:

- Diarrhea lasts more than two days in adults.
- Blood appears in stool.
- High fever develops.
- Severe dehydration occurs.

---

## Emergency Signs

Call emergency medical services immediately if:

- The person becomes unconscious.
- Severe dehydration develops.
- Continuous vomiting prevents drinking fluids.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is ORS important?

ORS replaces water and important salts lost during diarrhea.

### Should I stop eating?

No. Continue light meals unless advised otherwise by a healthcare professional.
